Questions

Before youget in touch.

Still wondering about something? Call (437) 608-1177 or send us a request.

Do you make custom jewelry?

Yes. Most of our work is bespoke. We start with a consultation about your idea, budget and how you'll wear it, then design the piece in CAD, size it to you and handcraft it at our Toronto atelier.

What gold do you use?

Every piece is made in-house in solid 18K or 22K gold. We never use plated metal, so the colour and weight last a lifetime.

Can I send a photo or an heirloom as a reference?

Yes. Add a reference image or PDF to the custom order form, or bring an heirloom piece to the atelier. We'll suggest how to recreate or reimagine it.

Can every piece be sized for me?

Yes. We offer custom sizing on every ring, chain and bracelet in the collection, as well as on bespoke pieces.

Do you offer aftercare?

Yes. Every Zarsa Gold piece comes with lifetime care, including complimentary cleaning and check-ups for as long as you own it.

How do I get a price?

Send a request through the custom order form, call (437) 608-1177 or email zarsagold@gmail.com. We reply with ideas, a timeline and a quote, usually within two business days.
